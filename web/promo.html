MonPetitRéseau est une petite app iPhone et iPad pour partager avec votre famille : un annuaire, un mur pour discuter, des photos, un calendrier d'événements, des listes de tâches.

Tout reste sur vos appareils. Pour échanger avec vos proches, l'app génère un lien magique que vous envoyez par SMS ou iMessage. Pas de compte à créer, pas de mot de passe.

Comment ça marche

  1. Vous créez votre famille et ajoutez vos proches.
  2. Vous touchez « Partager ma famille » et envoyez le lien par SMS.
  3. Vos proches l'ouvrent : leur app rejoint instantanément votre réseau.

Stay close, simply.

MonPetitRéseau is a small iPhone and iPad app to share with your family: a directory, a wall to chat, photos, an event calendar, shared task lists.

Everything stays on your devices. To exchange with relatives, the app generates a magic link you send by SMS or iMessage. No account, no password.

Features

👨‍👩‍👧

Directory

All your relatives, their birthdays, their family ties.

💬

Wall

A common timeline where everyone posts — like a real little network.

📸

Photos

A shared memory gallery that stays on your iPhones.

📅

Events

Family lunches, holidays, automatic birthdays.

✅

Tasks

Errands, paperwork: assign them to a relative.

🔒

Private

No third-party server. Your data is never sold. Ever.

How it works

  1. Create your family and add your relatives.
  2. Tap "Share my family" and send the link by SMS.
  3. Your relatives open it: their app joins your network instantly.
  4. Wall, photos, events and tasks sync across iPhones via iCloud.

Privacy by design

  • No server. No account. No tracking.
  • Your data lives on your iPhone and in the link you choose to send.
  • Sync between iPhones uses your own iCloud (Apple).
  • You stay in control: only the group creator can manage the member list.

Compatibility

iPhone and iPad — iOS 17 and above. Universal app, optimized for Liquid Glass.

Sync: requires an active iCloud account on the device.
